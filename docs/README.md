# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management guidance for OctoAcme. It gives the team a shared, lightweight operating model for how work is initiated, planned, executed, released, and improved over time.

## Project management overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative delivery model with clear accountability and measurable outcomes. Work begins with project initiation to confirm the problem, stakeholders, success metrics, and decision to move ahead. That is followed by planning to break the work into a prioritized backlog, define milestones, and identify risks and dependencies. During execution, the team delivers in small increments, tracks progress, and escalates blockers through defined ownership and communication channels. Releases are treated as controlled, observable events with validation, rollback readiness, and stakeholder communication. After each sprint or milestone, the team conducts retrospective reviews to capture learnings and convert them into concrete improvements.

## Typical lifecycle

1. Initiation: confirm the problem, stakeholders, and success criteria.
2. Planning: define scope, milestones, ownership, and risks.
3. Execution: build, test, review, and communicate progress.
4. Release: validate quality and deploy with rollback awareness.
5. Close & improvement: reflect, share learnings, and drive follow-up actions.

## How to use these docs

Use the overview and initiation guides for new work, move into planning when approval is granted, and rely on the execution, risk, release, and retrospective guides during delivery and review. Keep the information in this folder as the source of truth for team process and onboarding.
